Then run copypaw app to open the app in your browser.

How well does it match?

Each drawing's paws are compared with the pet's own clip: whether they land on the same beats, and whether paw height rises and falls in step. The timing words under a clip compare that match with the same drawing animated by other pets' motion. A "fair" or "strong" match beats 95 percent of those mismatched pairs, and "strong" also needs a high correlation. "Not measured" means the gallery has not been calibrated yet.

Pose accuracy will be measured on the APT-36K animal pose set. The evaluation page records what has been run so far, so this page does not repeat it.

Privacy

Copypaw runs on your own computer. Your drawing and your pet's clip are processed there and are not uploaded. The only download is the pose models, on the first run. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party script.

Your drawing and your clip stay yours, and so do the animations you make from them.1
